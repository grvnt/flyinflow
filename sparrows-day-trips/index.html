Cape Town City flying

Cape Town is known for dynamic thermic conditions, views of the City, and the Franschhoek Vineyards. On rare occasions, and only for advanced pilots, a flight from Table Mountain is possible. Lion's Head towers above the Atlantic, with a glide down to the landing field for wingovers at sunset — the crossing from Lion's Head to Table Mountain is always a highlight.

Outside city limits

Sir Lowry's Pass offers a long ridge along the Hottentot Holland range across False Bay. Franschhoek is close by and a fantastic thermic site — we usually fly until midday, then enjoy the afternoon at the valley's restaurants. Porterville is Cape Town's secret weapon for cross-country — flyable virtually every day.

Requirements

All pilots must arrange their own medical insurance and hold a valid IPPI card and SAHPA registration. Signal Hill requires an IPPI 5 card.

All guiding is in English. Every day has a morning and evening briefing to decide where we fly. Mondays are our day off — no guiding on Mondays.
