Birdmen has been guiding pilots around Cape Town's sites for years. Grant Smith, Birdmen Sparrows co-founder, instructor and guide, and WJ de Vries, Birdmen guide and Sparrows coach, between them hold multiple South African distance records and well over a thousand hours flying these mountains. Every guided day runs on that same local knowledge — the sites, the weather windows, and the people who know them best.

Meet your guides

Grant Smith

Birdmen Sparrows Co-Founder, Instructor & Guide

Grant realised his childhood dream of flying when he got his licence through Birdmen. After some years away from the sport, a chance meeting with Barry and the support of Birdmen brought him back — he's been flying ever since. Today Grant is dedicated to paragliding mastery and flow coaching, holding multiple site records including the longest foot-launched flight on an EN B glider in South Africa, and is one of only a handful of pilots to have flown over 200km in the Western Cape.

WJ de Vries

Birdmen Guide & Sparrows Coach

WJ's love for flying began with kites and parafoils as a kid, then skydiving — until mentorship from Birdmen introduced him to paragliding, where he found more joy in the canopy ride than the freefall. With over 11,700km and 950 flying hours, WJ holds the African XC distance record using 3 turnpoints at 276km, and coaches pilots on building confidence and making sound flying decisions.

Requirements

All pilots must arrange their own medical insurance and hold a valid IPPI card and SAHPA registration (temporary membership for visiting pilots — apply online at sahpa.co.za before your trip). Signal Hill requires an IPPI 5 card.

A South African SIM (Vodacom or MTN for best coverage) is recommended — our retrieve tracks your location over WhatsApp and works on an international number, it just costs more. All guiding is in English. Every day has a morning and evening briefing to decide where we fly. Mondays are our day off — no guiding on Mondays.
