Cape Town · Guided Days

Worldclass Paragliding for Every Type of Pilot

Guided day trips around Cape Town's best flying sites.

Requirements

All pilots must arrange their own medical insurance and hold a valid IPPI card and SAHPA registration (temporary membership for visiting pilots — apply online at sahpa.co.za before your trip). Signal Hill requires an IPPI 5 card.

A South African SIM (Vodacom or MTN for best coverage) is recommended — our retrieve tracks your location over WhatsApp and works on an international number, it just costs more. All guiding is in English. Every day has a morning and evening briefing to decide where we fly. Mondays are our day off — no guiding on Mondays.

Included

  • Vehicle and driver
  • Daily briefing
  • 3-day and 5-day weather forecasts
  • In-air radio coaching
  • Post-flight debrief
  • Fuel and daily site fees
  • Launch and landing assistance if required
  • Cape Town pickup (standard rate)
  • Lead-and-follow XC (standard rate)

Not included

  • SAHPA membership — apply at sahpa.co.za
  • Meals

Pricing
